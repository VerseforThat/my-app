2. The App and what it does

Verse for That helps you find a Bible verse that speaks to a problem or feeling you describe — through text input, voice dictation, or by browsing topics — and reads it aloud in a calming voice. Features include:

  • A daily verse on the home screen.
  • Verse matching for problems you describe in your own words.
  • A "deeper explanation" and "related verses" feature for any verse.
  • Voice playback of verses (powered by ElevenLabs).
  • Saved (favorited) verses, history of past matches, and personal notes (text and voice memos).
  • Search by reference (e.g. John 3:16) or topic.
  • Beautifully designed image-card sharing.
  • Optional daily 8 AM reminder notification.

The App is provided in King James Version (KJV) and New International Version (NIV) translations, selectable in Settings.
