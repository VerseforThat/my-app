Legal & Privacy

A Bible verse companion app by Wendy Ardolino.

Verse for That is a quiet, judgment-free space. When life gets hard, tell us what you're going through and we'll match a Bible verse to meet you there. Hear it spoken aloud, save what speaks to you, and revisit it whenever you need.
